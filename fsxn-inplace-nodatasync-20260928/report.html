Takeaways

  • The volume move was the only operation with a large, sustained impact: avg IOPS ~3860 with a ~682 floor during cutover, throughput roughly halved. Moving the ~470 GB-populated 1 TB constituent took 15m34s (cutover forced because active fio kept regenerating delta).
  • FlexGroup convert (36 s) caused only a brief single-interval dip.
  • HA expand ran non-disruptively — IOPS held ~4200; the client sees a spike to ~8000 right after the second aggregate comes online.
  • Expand ×16 and resize were fast metadata-level ops with minor short-lived impact; post-resize steady state (~4900 IOPS) was the highest of the run.
